# Photos

Philippine blood-donation photos used on the landing page and the sign-in screens.
All come from Wikimedia Commons under licences that need no attribution:
CC0 (public-domain dedication) or US government public domain. They were
cropped, lightly colour-corrected, and resized for the web.

Most are from a Philippine Red Cross (Bulacan Chapter) blood drive at SM City
Baliuag, February 2017, photographed by Judgefloro. `prc-blood-draw.jpg` is a
Philippine National Red Cross nurse, from a 2006 US Navy photo. `pgh-ward.jpg`
is a Philippine General Hospital ward photographed by the Department of Public
Works and Highways: a Philippine government work, which has no copyright
(RA 8293 s.176), though commercial use needs the agency's approval.

Before replacing any of these, prefer RedAgos's own photos of Davao facilities
(taken with permission). Keep this list current.
